# Transfer corridors — Bulgaria, Georgia, Andorra

Hand-researched 2026-08-10 for the package-math layer. **Every figure here is
an unverified estimate** gathered from operator listings and standard road
routing; prices are typical published ranges, not quotes, and winter mountain
conditions can add materially to every time below. Re-check before each
season, and never render a price from this file in the UI without the
estimate label.

Drive times/distances from this research are ALSO loaded into
`data/build/airport_transfers.json`, which the app reads (gateway airport +
"by road" line). Prices are NOT loaded anywhere — the app holds no transfer
prices yet; this table exists so the future per-person package estimate has a
sourced starting point.

## Bulgaria

| Corridor | Road | Typical options & price pp (checked 2026-08) |
|---|---|---|
| SOF → Bansko | ~160 km, ~2h30 | Shared shuttle €20–30; private car €55–75/vehicle (Traventuria and similar) |
| SOF → Borovets | ~72 km, ~1h15 | Shared shuttle €15–22; private €40–55/vehicle |
| PDV → Bansko | ~145 km, ~2h20 | Private €55–70/vehicle; fewer shared options |
| PDV → Pamporovo | ~85 km, ~1h30 | Shared €15–20; private €40–55/vehicle |
| SOF → Pamporovo | ~235 km, ~3h10 | Private €85–110/vehicle; PDV is the better gateway |
| SOF → Vitosha (Aleko) | ~25 km, ~40 min | City taxi/rideshare €12–20; public bus + gondola |

## Georgia

| Corridor | Road | Typical options & price pp (checked 2026-08) |
|---|---|---|
| TBS → Gudauri | ~120 km, ~2h | Shared minivan (marshrutka) 15–25 GEL; GoTrip/private car 150–250 GEL/vehicle. Georgian Military Highway — short weather closures happen |
| TBS → Bakuriani | ~180 km, ~2h50 | Private 200–300 GEL/vehicle; slow rail option via Borjomi exists |
| KUT → Bakuriani | ~205 km, ~3h | Private 250–350 GEL/vehicle; KUT is the Wizz Air low-cost gateway |
| KUT → Mestia (Hatsvali/Tetnuldi) | ~135 km, ~3h | Shared minivan 30–50 GEL; private 250–400 GEL/vehicle; mountain road, 4x4 advisable in winter |
| BUS → Goderdzi | ~105 km, ~2h30 | Private/4x4 150–300 GEL; upper section unpaved — operator shuttle or 4x4 in winter |

## Andorra (no in-country airport)

| Corridor | Road | Typical options & price pp (checked 2026-08) |
|---|---|---|
| BCN → Grandvalira / Andorra la Vella | ~200 km, ~3h | Andbus/Directbus coach €35–40 one-way; private €190–250/vehicle |
| TLS → Andorra | ~180 km, ~2h45 | Andbus coach €35–40 one-way; fewer daily departures than BCN |
| Andorra la Vella → Pal-Arinsal | ~10 km | Local bus/taxi; trivial add-on |
| Andorra la Vella → Ordino-Arcalís | ~18 km | Local bus/taxi; Arcalís is a day-trip mountain — most sleep in La Massana or Ordino |
