## 6b. Curated overrides and derived aliases

Source: `data/seed/area_overrides.json`

OpenSkiMap clusters runs and lifts geometrically, so two separately-marketed resorts
that share lift-linked terrain become ONE record under ONE name. We add aliases so the
record is findable under either name, and attach a boundary note the UI must show next

faithful basis for that split, and a guessed piste figure is exactly the fabrication

## 6c. Border-spanning areas

`country` is the primary, for display. `countries` holds every country the area
touches, primary first, and country FILTERS match on any of them — a border resort
genuinely belongs to both sides, and someone flying into Innsbruck for Drei Zinnen
must be able to find it under Austria.

Upstream `places` entries whose `iso3166_1Alpha2` contradicts the country prefix of
`iso3166_2` are discarded before any country is chosen — they are noise, and they
misassign real resorts. This run discarded **12** such entries.

## 6d. Resort names that collide with ordinary words

Core pins a result when the query names a resort — typing "Val Gardena" pins the Alta
Badia record via its aliases. A resort whose whole name is also an ordinary word breaks
that: Georgia has an area called **Crystal**, so "crystal clear snow for beginners"
pinned a Georgian resort to a query that was not about it.

Folded all **2421** distinct single-word names/aliases of at least
4 letters (matching core's `MIN_NAME_MATCH_CHARS`) against a
246-word ordinary-skier-vocabulary list.

**1 collision found.**
Each one below must appear in `AMBIGUOUS_SINGLE_WORD_NAMES` in
`packages/core/src/scoring.ts`, or it will pin on ordinary prose.

| Word | Name/alias forms | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| `crystal` | Crystal | Crystal [GE] |

This list is only correct for the countries currently in the build. Adding Austria,
France or Switzerland — and above all any English-speaking market, where *Sunshine*,
*Paradise*, *Panorama*, *Powder* and *Summit* are all real resort names — will
introduce new collisions. That is why this is recomputed every build rather than
hand-maintained. The vocabulary list is a heuristic (`src/nameCollisions.ts`): it will
miss words, and a hit here is a prompt to look, not proof of a bug.

## 8. Upstream surprises

- Difficulty keys upstream emits that `DIFFICULTIES` in core does not have: `extreme` (×2). Their km counts toward `kmTotal` but they are excluded from `runsByDifficulty` and `terrainMix`, exactly like `other`.
- Ski-area geometry in the target countries: Point ×2457, MultiPolygon ×29, Polygon ×1243. Polygons are reduced to a centre-of-mass point, because `ski_area.location` is `geography(Point,4326)`.
- 4 downhill areas worldwide carry no country in `places` at all.
- Upstream lift types use HYPHENS (`t-bar`, `j-bar`) where `LIFT_CAPACITY_PPH` uses underscores (`t_bar`, `j_bar`), so those lifts are silently taking the `unknown` fallback. Add the hyphenated spellings to the table. See section 7.
- **`snowmakingKm` is effectively unavailable.** Of 108,612 downhill runs worldwide, only 772 carry `piste:snowmaking=yes` (0.7%) and 107,714 are untagged. In the target countries only 596 runs are tagged at all. The area-level `snowmakingLengthInKm` statistic is correspondingly empty. **`snowmakingKm: 0` therefore means "not tagged", NOT "no snowmaking"** — the model types it as a non-nullable number so the unknown cannot be expressed as null. PLAN.md §5 plans to build the "snow-reliable" signal on this tag; it cannot be built from this dump. Use altitude and aspect instead, and do not let the scorer read `snowmakingKm`.
- `lit` by contrast is genuinely usable: 5,966 yes / 14,107 explicit no / 88,539 untagged (18.5% of downhill runs carry a real value). That is why night skiing gets a tri-state and snowmaking does not get a signal at all.
- 9,544 downhill runs worldwide have an EMPTY `skiAreas` array — they belong to no ski area upstream and cannot be attributed to one. Any per-area run aggregate built from runs.geojson is missing that share.
